# Week 6: Integrative Muscle Structure and Function — Organ, Limb, and Whole-Organism Levels

This week extends the muscle physiology unit from the cellular and tissue scales of Week 5 up through the organ, limb, and whole-organism scales of locomotion.

## Lectures

- [**Lecture 13: Integrative Muscle Structure and Function 3 — Organ and Limb Level**](lecture13-muscle-organ-limb) (37 slides)
   Recap of intrinsic tissue properties; muscle-tendon architecture (parallel, pennate, multipennate, PCSA); architectural force-length and force-velocity trade-offs; tendon length and stiffness ratios; lever systems and torque balance; effective mechanical advantage and its scaling with body size.

- [**Lecture 14: Integrative Muscle Function 4 — Limb and Whole-Organism Level**](lecture14-muscle-whole-organism) (37 slides)
   Inverse dynamics and the proximo-distal gradient in cursorial limbs, in vivo measurement with sonomicrometry and tendon buckles, muscle work loops, case studies in turkey, wallaby, guinea fowl, and cockatiel, the flow-of-energy framework (motor, spring, brake), in vivo ultrasound in human walking and running, and applications to prosthetics and exoskeletons.
