# Week 8: Energy Cost of Locomotion and Humans in Comparative Context

This week completes the locomotion unit by connecting **force demands** to the **metabolic energy cost** of moving, and then places **human locomotion** in an evolutionary and comparative framework.

## Lectures

- [**Lecture 17: Forces and Energy Cost in Locomotion (Part 2)**](lecture17-energy-cost) (35 slides)
   Walking ground reaction forces; scaling of strength, posture, and EMA with body size; respirometry, the Fick principle, and mass-specific cost of transport; the Kram & Taylor 1/T<sub>c</sub> and 1/L<sub>c</sub> framework; gait selection in quadrupeds and bipeds; reduced-gravity experiments and real-world wearable studies.

- [**Lecture 18: Putting Human Locomotion into a Comparative Context**](lecture18-humans-comparative) (41 slides)
   Reading cost-of-transport curves; convergent morphological and physiological adaptations for endurance and speed; factorial aerobic scope; the Bramble & Lieberman endurance-running hypothesis; modern Rubenson and Pontzer reappraisals; barefoot vs shod running; head stability and the brain-evolution hypothesis.
